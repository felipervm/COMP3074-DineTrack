# DineTrack — Prototype 1 Video Script

Suggested duration: 4–5 minutes.
Keep the emulator readable and demonstrate each action as you explain it.

## 1. Introduction and Splash

Action: Start recording before launching the app. Show the Splash screen and its automatic transition to the restaurant list.

Say:
“Hello, my name is Felipe da Rocha Vieira Mattos, student ID 101581203, Team P 41. This is DineTrack, my individual project for COMP3074, Mobile Application Development I. Today I will demonstrate Prototype 1, focusing on the user interface.”

## 2. Restaurant List

Action: Show Bar Isabel, Pizzeria Libretto, and Pai. Scroll through their cards to show addresses, ratings, and tags.

Say:
“DineTrack is a personal restaurant guide that helps users save, organize, rate, and revisit restaurants. The main screen displays saved restaurants with their addresses, ratings, and tags. The app uses a consistent Material 3 theme with warm backgrounds, dark green accents, and rounded cards.”

## 3. Search and Filter

Action: Open Search and Filter. Search for Pai, then Pizza. Clear the query and select a tag filter. Choose All tags, then search for zzz to show the empty state.

Say:
“The search screen filters restaurants by name or tag. For example, I can search for Pai by name or Pizza by tag. I can also select a tag filter. When nothing matches, the app displays a helpful empty state.”

## 4. Restaurant Details and Share

Action: Clear the query and select All tags. Open a restaurant. Show its fields, tap Share, and dismiss the preview.

Say:
“Selecting a restaurant opens its details, including the rating, address, phone number, description, and tags. The Share button displays a preview for this UI milestone.”

## 5. Map and Directions

Action: Open Map and Directions. Tap Get Directions, dismiss the dialog, and return to Details.

Say:
“The Map and Directions screen shows a visual preview of the planned feature and the restaurant’s destination. Live Google Maps, GPS, and route guidance are planned for the functional prototype.”

## 6. Add Restaurant

Action: Return to the restaurant list and tap Add Restaurant. Attempt to save with a blank name. Then complete the form using the sample below and save. Show the new card in the list.

Sample data:
- Name: Demo Café
- Address: 100 King St, Toronto
- Phone: 416-555-0100
- Description: A cozy café to visit with friends.
- Tags: Café, Brunch
- Rating: 4 stars

Say:
“The form includes all planned restaurant fields. First, I will try saving without a name to demonstrate required-field validation. Now I will complete the form and save. The new restaurant is added to the in-memory list.”

## 7. Edit Restaurant

Action: Open Demo Café, tap Edit, change its rating to five stars, and save. Show the updated rating.

Say:
“I can also edit an existing restaurant. Here, I am updating its rating to five stars. After saving, the details show the updated information.”

## 8. Delete Restaurant

Action: Tap Delete Restaurant and cancel once. Tap Delete Restaurant again and confirm. Show that Demo Café has disappeared from the list.

Say:
“Deleting a restaurant requires confirmation. I can cancel to keep the entry, or confirm to remove it. The restaurant now disappears from the list.”

## 9. About and Closing

Action: Open About. Show the course, team, full name, and student ID. Use the system back button to return to the restaurant list.

Say:
“The About screen displays the app description, course, team, my name, and student ID. All seven planned screens are included, with working navigation, search, add, edit, and delete interactions. This prototype uses in-memory data, which resets when the app process restarts. Thank you for watching.”

## Before Recording

- Complete the entire sequence once to confirm every interaction works without crashing.
- Keep the emulator large enough for text to be readable.
- Start recording before launching the app so the Splash screen appears.
- Demonstrate the add, edit, and delete steps in the same session because entries are stored in memory.
